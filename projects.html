Interpreting Deepfake Detection on SID-Set

January-April 2026

Benchmarked frozen CLIP, DINOv2, and SigLIP; a CLIP linear probe achieved 90.0% macro-F1. Investigated detection cues with SHAP, Token-CAM, and layer-wise probing, and compared five sparse-autoencoder variants on SIDA-7B embeddings using ablation and causal steering.

Mapped confidence-stratified and adversarial failure modes, including 12.7% accuracy on the lowest-confidence 20% of tampered evaluation images.

GitHub

CLALM: Continual Multilingual Visual Question Answering

August-November 2025

Developed a continual multilingual VQA pipeline with Qwen3-VL-2B and LoRA on ALM-Bench across 12 languages. Compared sequential fine-tuning with online EWC under clustered, interleaved, and randomized language orders.

Evaluated accuracy, exact match, and token-level F1 by language and question type, with a Gradio demonstration for comparing trained adapters.

GitHub

ControlNet Reproduction and Spatially Conditioned Generation

March-April 2026

Reproduced ControlNet over frozen Stable Diffusion 1.5, implementing zero convolutions, the conditioning encoder, a trainable UNet encoder copy, and skip-connection injection from scratch.

Trained on Fill50K; evaluated FID, CLIP Score, and PSNR, and investigated encoder-freezing configurations and the onset of conditioning.
